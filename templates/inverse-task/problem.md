# Gravitationally coupled coherent states

Consider two harmonic oscillators of equal mass $m$, equal angular frequency $\omega$,
separated by a distance $d$, and initially in the coherent state
$\ket{\alpha} \otimes \ket{-\alpha}$ for $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$.
The oscillators interact gravitationally, and the quadratic approximation to
the Hamiltonian
$H = \sum_{i=1,2} \left(\frac{p_i^2}{2m} + \frac{m}{2}\omega^2 x_i^2\right) + \frac{2Gm^2}{d^3} x_1 x_2$
is assumed to be exactly valid.

The angular frequency is $\omega = 1/\mathrm{s}$ and the distance is one micron.
Take the gravitational constant as exactly $G = 6.6743 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m^3 kg^{-1} s^{-2}}$.


# What you can use

You have access to the position expectation value of the first oscillator at

Budgeted probes may be used at most **4** times in total.

# Your task

Find the mass $m$ of the oscillators.

# Output format

State the mass in micrograms, rounded to two decimal places. State only the
numerical value without units and without other formatting. Example: 1.23
