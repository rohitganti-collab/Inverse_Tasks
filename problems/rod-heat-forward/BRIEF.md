# BRIEF — driven rod, peak probe temperature

> **Reference fixture — do not submit.** A complete forward task, kept so the
> engine has a forward problem to test against and so experts can read one
> before writing their own.

One paragraph, in the author's words:

> A rod is heated by a buried heater whose output pulses up and down on a fixed
> period. Both ends are held cold. A sensor part-way along the rod reads a
> temperature that, after a while, repeats the same shape every cycle. The
> question asks for the highest reading that sensor ever takes. All the physics
> is handed over — diffusivity, geometry, source, boundary conditions — so
> nothing is hidden and nothing has to be inferred. The whole task is whether
> the solver notices that a maximum is not an average, and actually marches the
> problem in time instead of dropping the time derivative and solving the much
> easier steady problem that the setup invites.
